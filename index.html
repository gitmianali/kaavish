The band

Kaavish is a contemporary semi classical band from Pakistan formed in 1998 comprising of Jaffer Zaidi & Maaz Maudood. The band is known for their songs Bachpan, Tere pyaar mein, Moray Sayyaan, Nindiya re, Faasle among others. Their debut album Gunkali earned widespread critical acclaim for its poetic expression, refined production, and melodic sophistication.
